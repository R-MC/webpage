Spearfishing

What it is

Spearfishing is hunting fish underwater with a spear or speargun, on a single breath. No tank, no air. You swim down, wait, and come back up. It is closer to hunting than it is to angling — you have to get near the fish, and you only get as long as your lungs allow.

Why I like it

It is quiet. Once you are a few metres down there is no noise at all, and everything slows down. It is also the most honest way I know of to catch something: you take one fish, the one you chose, and nothing else gets hurt. You cannot leave a net behind by accident.

The one thing people get wrong

Weighting. Too much lead and you sink when you are tired, which is exactly when you least want to. Most beginners wear far too much of it.

A note on the flag

A dive flag is not optional. It tells boats a person is in the water.

Small print

Rules vary by province, so check local regulations before going out.
